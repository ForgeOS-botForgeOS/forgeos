ForgeOS — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026 · Applies to the ForgeOS web app and the ForgeOS Android app.

The short version. ForgeOS keeps your training, food and health data on your own device. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking of any kind — the app contains no advertising or analytics SDK at all. Data leaves your device only for features you switch on: signing in, syncing with friends, scanning a photo or barcode, or asking the AI trainer. You can export everything and delete everything from inside the app.

Who is responsible

ForgeOS is an independent, non-commercial project developed in Slovakia (EU). For anything in this policy — including a request to see or delete your data — contact us through the project's issue tracker at github.com/ForgeOS-botForgeOS/forgeos/issues, or by using the feedback link inside the app (You → “Found a bug, or want something added?”).

What is stored on your device

By default ForgeOS is a local app. The following is written to your device's own storage and to nowhere else unless a section below says otherwise:

  • Your profile: name, sex, age, height, weight, goal, experience, and the answers you gave during onboarding.
  • Training: workouts, exercises, sets, weights, reps, RPE, notes, personal records, streaks, XP and achievements.
  • Nutrition: what you logged, water, saved meals, and the corrections you made to scanned results.
  • Health and recovery: sleep, steps, resting heart rate and active calories, if you connect Health Connect.
  • Body data: weigh-ins, measurements and any progress photos you take (photos never leave the device).
  • Settings, including your app-lock passcode — stored only as a salted hash, never as the digits you typed.

If you never turn any of these on, ForgeOS makes no network requests that carry your data.

Device permissions

  • Camera — only while a scanner screen is open, to read a barcode or take a meal photo. Nothing is recorded or uploaded except a photo you deliberately scan.
  • Health Connect (read-only) — sleep, steps, resting heart rate and active calories, so the app can show readiness. It is read from your phone; it is uploaded only if you turn cloud sync on.
  • Notifications — the morning readiness note and reminders you set.
  • Location — used only by the optional “gym check-in” feature, which is off by default. Coordinates are compared on your device and never sent anywhere.
  • Install packages — so the app can hand a downloaded update to the Android installer. You confirm every install.

Children and teenagers

ForgeOS is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you are under 18, please show this policy to a parent or guardian before turning on any feature that sends data off your device — particularly the AI trainer. The app is a training tool, not a medical service, and it does not give medical advice.

Legal basis and retention (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, our legal basis is your consent, given by turning a feature on; and, for the parts you have enabled, the performance of the service you asked for. Data kept in our backend stays until you delete it or delete your account; local data stays until you delete the app or clear it in the app. Feedback messages are kept while the reported problem is open.

Your rights, and how to use them

  • See and export everything: You → Backup → Export. It produces a complete JSON file of your data.
  • Delete everything: You → Reset (removes local data). If you created an account, ask us via the issue tracker or the in-app feedback link and we will delete the account and all rows belonging to it.
  • Withdraw consent: every off-device feature has its own switch in You → Preferences, and the AI trainer has its own consent screen you can withdraw at any time.
  • You also have the right to object, to restrict processing, and to complain to your supervisory authority (in Slovakia, the Office for Personal Data Protection).

Security

Traffic is HTTPS only. Backend rows are protected by row-level security so an account can only reach its own data (and a friend's, only after that friend accepts and only while they have sharing on). The app ships a strict Content-Security-Policy, refuses to run inside another site, and never contains a private API key — the repository is public, so anything shipped to the app would be public too.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the previous versions remain in the project's public git history.
