ForgeOS — Terms of Use

Last updated: 4 September 2026

The short version. ForgeOS is a free training app made by one person. Use it to train, not to replace a doctor or a coach. Be decent to the people you connect with. It comes as-is, and your data is yours — export it any time.

1. What ForgeOS is

ForgeOS is a free fitness, nutrition and habit app for personal, non-commercial use, provided as a web app and an Android app. There is no subscription, no advertising and nothing to buy. In-app coins and cosmetics are earned in the app, have no monetary value, and cannot be bought, sold or exchanged.

2. Not medical or professional advice

Everything in the app — training plans, calorie and macro targets, readiness scores, the AI trainer, recipes and supplement notes — is general information, produced by software. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and it is not a substitute for a doctor, dietitian or qualified coach. Talk to a professional before starting a new training or nutrition programme, and especially if you have an injury, a health condition, are pregnant, or are under 18. If something hurts, stop. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Nutrition figures from photo scanning, barcode lookups and food databases are estimates from third-party data and AI models. They can be wrong. Check anything that matters.

3. The AI trainer

The AI trainer is optional and asks for explicit consent before it sends anything off your device. It is a language model: it can be confidently wrong, and it will refuse subjects it should not handle. Do not rely on it for medical, psychological or emergency questions. If you are struggling, the app will point you to real help — please use it.

4. Your account and your data

You can use ForgeOS entirely offline with no account. If you create one, keep your credentials to yourself; you are responsible for what happens under your account. You keep ownership of everything you put into the app. You can export all of it (You → Backup → Export) and delete it at any time. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored where.

5. Being decent

The social parts of the app — the feed, friends, races, duels, the plan marketplace and feedback — are shared with real people. Do not post content that is illegal, hateful, harassing, sexual, or that impersonates somebody else; do not upload anything you do not have the right to share; do not scrape, spam, or attack the service or other users; and do not try to break, overload or reverse-engineer the backend or the AI endpoints. We can remove content and end access for accounts that do these things.

6. Availability

ForgeOS is a hobby project running on free infrastructure. Features that depend on outside services — sign-in, sync, AI scanning, the trainer, music — can be slow, rate-limited or unavailable, and may change or disappear. The app is built to keep working offline when they do. Nothing here is a guarantee of uptime.
